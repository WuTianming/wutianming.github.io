I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/), advised by [Prof. Dixin Tang](https://dx-tang.github.io/) in the [UT Data Systems Lab](https://ut-datasys.github.io/). My research is on data systems, with a current focus on efficient systems for billion-scale vector databases.

Before UT, I was a research assistant at Yale University, and I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/) (USTC). I have also worked on LLM inference under memory constraints as a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia, and on out-of-core graph partitioning for GNN training at USTC's Advanced Data Systems Laboratory.
